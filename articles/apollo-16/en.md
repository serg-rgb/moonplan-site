According to NASA's description, Apollo 16 was the first exploration of the lunar highlands: John Young and Charles Duke landed in the Descartes highlands. Just before the landing, a fault was found in the engine control system of the service module, and the landing was postponed by almost six hours.

## The crew

Commander John Young, 41, was flying into space for the fourth time (before that, Gemini 3, Gemini 10 and Apollo 10). Command module pilot Thomas Mattingly, 36, and lunar module pilot Charles Duke, 36, were flying for the first time. The command module was called Casper and the lunar module Orion.

## Launch

The launch was first planned for March 17 but was postponed because of a fault in the docking ring jettison mechanism. The Saturn V lifted off on April 16, 1972 at 17:54 UTC.

[[figure:launch]]

On the way to the Moon, another problem was found: a false signal could throw off the navigation system of the command module. It was solved by changing the onboard computer program in flight.

## Six hours of waiting

On April 20 at 18:08 UTC Orion separated from Casper. But before the landing it turned out that the control system of the service module's main engine was not working properly: the engine began to swing. The landing was postponed by almost six hours. Engineers on the ground decided that the fault would not prevent the spacecraft from being controlled, and allowed the mission to continue.

From launch to splashdown, 265 hours 51 minutes 5 seconds passed.

## On the surface

Orion landed on April 21 at 02:23:35 UTC in the Descartes highlands, north of Dolland crater, at 9.0° S, 15.5° E. Young and Duke went out onto the surface three times, for 20 hours 14 minutes in total. In the lunar rover they drove 27 km, collected 95.71 kg of rock and soil, set up the ALSEP science instruments and took many photographs.

## What the Moon looked like from Earth that day

We calculated the phase for the moment of landing, April 21, 1972 at 02:23:35 UTC. From Earth the Moon was waxing and 56% lit, slightly more than first quarter: 7.24 days had passed since the new moon on April 13. The distance from the centre of the Earth to the centre of the Moon was 386,765 km.

## Home a day early

Because of the fault in the main engine, the expedition was cut short by a day. The small science satellite that was released into lunar orbit ended up in an elongated orbit, where it could operate for about a month instead of the planned year. On April 25 Mattingly made a spacewalk and, in two trips to the scientific instrument bay, retrieved the film cassettes.

Apollo 16 splashed down on April 27 at 19:45:05 UTC in the central Pacific Ocean, 215 miles southeast of Christmas Island and 5 km from the USS Ticonderoga. The Casper command module is on display at the Space and Rocket Center in Huntsville, Alabama.
