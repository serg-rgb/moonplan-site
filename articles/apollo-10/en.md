Apollo 10 went the whole way of Apollo 11, except for the last kilometers. As NASA describes it, this was a full "dry run": the crew carried out every operation of a lunar expedition except the landing itself.

## The crew and call signs

Commander Thomas Stafford, command module pilot John Young and lunar module pilot Eugene Cernan. The crew named the spacecraft after characters from the Peanuts comic strip: the command and service module was Charlie Brown, and the lunar module was Snoopy.

## The launch

The Saturn V lifted off on May 18, 1969 at 16:49 UTC from Launch Complex 39B.

## Flight timeline

| Date and time, UTC | Event |
|---|---|
| May 18, 16:49 | Launch from Complex 39B |
| May 21, 20:44:54 | Entry into lunar orbit, 315.5 × 110.4 km |
| May 22, 19:36:17 | Snoopy separates from Charlie Brown |
| May 23, 03:22 | Re-docking |
| May 24, 10:25:29 | After 31 orbits the engine sends the spacecraft toward Earth |
| May 26, 16:52:23 | Splashdown in the Pacific Ocean |

From launch to splashdown, 192 hours 3 minutes 23 seconds passed, eight days.

## Snoopy near the surface

On May 22 Stafford and Cernan moved into the lunar module and separated from the command module, where Young stayed. Snoopy descended over Landing Site 2 in the Sea of Tranquility, the site chosen for Apollo 11. NSSDCA writes that at its closest point the module was about 14 km from the Moon; NASA articles give 47,000 feet, which is about 14.3 km, and 8.4 nautical miles, about 15.6 km.

[[figure:site-2]]

Snoopy's independent flight lasted 8 hours 10 minutes, and the module moved away from Charlie Brown by as much as 390 miles. NSSDCA notes a malfunction in the lunar module's abort guidance system. At 03:22 UTC on May 23 the ascent stage docked with the command module again.

[[figure:ascent]]

## The return and the speed record

On reentry Apollo 10 was traveling at 24,791 miles per hour, almost 39,900 km/h. NASA calls this the fastest atmospheric entry of any crewed flight. The command module splashed down on May 26, 1969 at 16:52:23 UTC in the Pacific Ocean, east of American Samoa, a few kilometers from the aircraft carrier USS Princeton.

## What the Moon looked like from Earth

We calculated the phase for two moments. At launch, May 18, 1969 at 16:49 UTC, the Moon was a thin waxing crescent, 5% illuminated, 2.3 days after the new moon. When Snoopy separated from Charlie Brown, on May 22 at 19:36 UTC, a waxing Moon, 34% illuminated, was visible from Earth.
